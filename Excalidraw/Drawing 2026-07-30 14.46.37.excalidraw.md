# My Understanding/The Requirements manually written out
- I'm not sure how we want to design it, but we need to be able to mark the highest tier someone reached (to allow us to choose what they will be the next year, and determine if they should receive the tier achievement award if they re-hit that tier after a return), and also mark the highest YTD spend reach (maybe it's the highest PRO Bucks milestone reached -- the goal is to make sure we know not to give them more PRO Bucks that they have already earned -- YTD spend probably makes sense, but I will defer to what you think is best design).
- I don't believe we need a rebate watermark, because we do decrement rebates for returns (unless a sale happens before the payout of rebates, and the return happens after the payout...we can't decrement their rebates...unless we allow a negative amount...leave this as an open question to ask Sabrina Rabsatt)
- You can only elect for rebates vs PRO Bucks once per year. If you achieve gold tier during a year before Nov 1, your rebate choice will be locked-in for the rest of that year. When the Jan 1 tier re-evaluation logic runs, you will be placed in the highest tier you achieved from the last year (Gold in this case). you will have to re-elect your benefits. If you achieve gold tier during a year AFTER Nov 1, you will not be allowed to re-elect PRO Bucks vs rebates. If you achieve diamond sometime during the year, you still won't re-elect because you already elected when you hit gold (either during the year or at the tier re-evaluation period on Jan 1). Only one election per year.
- Lookbacks during beta go 12 months from seeding
- after t&c acceptance, you get ytd spend from now back until the beginning of the year
- on Jan 1, evaluate what new year tier is going to be based on the highest tier reached from previous year (not ytd spend, which takes into account returns). If you crossed a tier threshold, even if you returned the sale that got you there, you will be in that tier in the next year.
- when a rebate is payed out, your rebate balance should go to $0.
- Beta Rebate payouts are for periods from Oct 1, 2026 - Jan 31, 2027, First semi-annual rebate period, from Feb 1 - Jun 30 2027, Second is July 1 - December 31 2027, then we get into a normal routine of Jan 1 - June 30, and July 1 - Dec 31 forever more.
- If you make a sale that puts you over the line from silver to gold, anything awards given over the threshold need to be "held"/not awarded until you choose your election (rebate vs PRO Bucks), then you are awarded them. If you choose PRO bucks, your held tier achievement award is awarded, and if you crossed a $1000 threshold after reaching the next tier, your held PRO Bucks is awarded. If you choose rebates, your held tier achievement award is given in full as a rebate, and the remaining money earned over the tier is calculated and awarded.
- If you return a sale before the payout of a rebate, your rebate balance will be decremented.
- Tier achievement rebates awarded are not clawed back.
- If you return a sale that earned you PRO Bucks when you've elected PRO Bucks (not rebate), you will not have that clawed back. The effects of your return will appear in your required spend to the next award milestone (e.g. If you had 7500 YTD spend and you were in the Silver Tier, if you spend $500 more dollars, you get $50 PRO Bucks (when you hit $8000). Lets say you spend $600 and YTD spend is $8100 (your high watermark). You reached the $8k milestone and got the PRO Bucks for it. your next milestone is $9k. If you return $500 of that $600 spend, you are now at $7600 YTD spend, but your next milestone is still $9k, so you need to spend $1400 to get to your next tier).
- We need to have a fraud conversation to check for cases where a person is getting high tiers each year by purchasing, picking up, and returning big sales (amongst other cases).
